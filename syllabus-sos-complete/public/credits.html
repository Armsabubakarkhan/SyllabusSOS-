Credits

You see the price before you book.

Prices are shown in credits. Nothing is charged by surprise.

How credits work

Credits move in four steps: held, paid, refunded or reviewed.

Held, then paid
When you request a session your credits move into a hold. The tutor receives them after you confirm the session, or automatically 72 hours after it ends if nobody raised a problem.
Platform fee
A small fee is taken from the tutor's payment. It is shown before you book.
Refunds
Declined, cancelled and expired requests return every credit to you.
Problems
If a session goes wrong, either side can raise it. An administrator reads both sides and decides on a refund or a release.
Demo credits
Credits in this version are for demonstration and have no cash value.

Privacy policy

Pilot version for the Agentathon 2026 prototype. Have a qualified lawyer review this policy against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 before any public launch.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, university, department, year of study, role and the short bio you choose to write.
  • Activity you create: tutoring listings, session requests, reviews, messages, friend requests, posts and comments.
  • Wallet records: credit holds, payments, refunds, fees and withdrawal requests. We never collect card numbers or bank passwords.
  • Security records: your IP address and browser type when you sign in or change something important, kept in an audit log to protect accounts and settle disputes.

Why we use it

To run your account, show tutors and learners to each other, hold and release credits, deliver messages, keep the service safe and resolve reports and disputes. We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

Who can see what

  • Your name, role, department, year, bio, listings and reviews are visible to signed-in members of your own university only.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members. Administrators can see it.
  • Messages are visible to you and the person you message, and to administrators if a message is reported.
  • Sessions held online use a meeting link on a video service such as Jitsi, Zoom or Google Meet. When you open the link, that service's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in. It is not readable by scripts and is not used for tracking. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions last at most 7 days.
  • When you delete your account, your profile is anonymised, your messages, friend links and comments are deleted and your listings are removed.
  • Credit records and audit entries are kept after deletion because financial and dispute records must stay accurate. They no longer show your name.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time from the Profile page.
  • Delete your account from the Profile page. Finish open sessions and use or withdraw your credits first.
  • Ask to see or correct your data by using the report button and choosing your own profile.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes. Connections are encrypted when the site is served over HTTPS. Access to data is limited by role and by university. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Age

The service is for people aged 18 or older, or younger people whose parent or guardian has consented where the law requires it.

Contact

Send privacy questions and complaints with the report button inside the app. The operator must add a named grievance contact here before launch.

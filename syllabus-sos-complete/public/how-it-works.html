How it works

From a question in your course to a finished session.

Every step happens inside your own university, so you are dealing with people from your own classes.

For a student who needs help

Type the course you are stuck in and see who at your university can teach it.

  1. Find someone in your courseType the course name. Students and teachers at your university can both offer sessions.
  2. Request a timePick a date and length. Your credits are held by the platform and are not sent to the tutor yet.
  3. MeetOnline with a meeting link made on the site, in person at a place you agree, or by private message.
  4. Confirm the sessionYou confirm it happened and the tutor is paid. If something went wrong, raise a problem and an administrator reviews it.
  5. Leave a reviewRate the session so the next student can choose well.

For a student or teacher who wants to teach

Students and teachers have their own space after signing in, each with the tools that fit their role.

  1. Offer a courseType the course you can teach, set a price in credits and say when you are free. If the course is new at your university, it is added for you.
  2. Answer requestsAccept or decline each request. Accepting makes a meeting link for you.
  3. Get paidCredits are released to you when the learner confirms, or 72 hours after the session if nobody raises a problem.

More than tutoring

Add classmates as friends, message them privately, and use your campus feed to share events, hackathons, study groups and opportunities. Students and teachers can both post an event for their campus.

Teachers review shared resources, see which topics students find hard, and post announcements once an administrator has verified them. Seniors can hand over what helped them in a course.

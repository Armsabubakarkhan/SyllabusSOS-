Terms of service

Pilot version for the Agentathon 2026 prototype. Have a qualified lawyer review these terms against Indian law before any public launch.

1. What SyllabusSOS++ is

SyllabusSOS++ connects students and teachers of the same university so they can arrange one on one study sessions, message each other and share campus events. SyllabusSOS++ is a platform. It is not a school, and it does not employ tutors or guarantee any academic result.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older, or have the consent of a parent or guardian where the law requires it.
  • Give accurate details and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Choose the university you actually study or teach at. Accounts that misrepresent this may be removed.
  • One person, one account.

3. Sessions between users

A session is an agreement between a learner and a tutor. Tutors set their own prices and formats. Learners and tutors are responsible for turning up on time and behaving respectfully. For in person sessions, meet in a public place on campus and tell someone where you are going.

4. Credits, fees and refunds

  • Prices are shown in credits. When you request a session, the full price is held by the platform.
  • If the tutor declines, or either side cancels before the session is confirmed, the held credits return to the learner.
  • After a session, the learner confirms it. The tutor then receives the price minus the platform fee shown before booking.
  • If nobody confirms or disputes a session within 72 hours of its end, the platform releases payment to the tutor.
  • If a problem is raised, an administrator reviews it and decides on a refund or release. That decision is final inside the platform.
  • In the demonstration version, credits have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn as money. Before real money is handled, a licensed payment provider and the applicable Indian payment, tax and consumer rules must be in place.

5. Academic honesty and acceptable use

  • Use tutoring to learn. Do not buy or sell completed assignments, exam answers or impersonation in an assessment.
  • No harassment, hate, threats, spam, adult content or illegal content.
  • Do not upload or share material you do not have the right to share.
  • Do not probe, overload or attempt to break into the service or other accounts.
